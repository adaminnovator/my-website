Device Power Consumption Calculation for EU Commercial Vehicles

1. Power Consumption Specifications

  • AOV mode: approx. 500 mW
  • Typical power consumption (with SD cards installed + SIM card dial-up): approx. 3.2 W
  • Full-load power consumption (SD cards + SIM dial-up + 4G streaming media preview + WiFi mobile connection + video preview): approx. 5 W
Note: The above data are laboratory test results under specific conditions. Actual values may vary due to product differences, operating environment and test methods.

2. Standard Starting Batteries for EU Commercial Vehicles

  • Light commercial van / truck: 12 V system (80 Ah / 100 Ah / 120 Ah)
  • Heavy-duty truck: 24 V system (100 Ah / 180 Ah / 225 Ah)

3. Calculation Formula

Operating Time (H) = Battery Voltage (V) × Battery Capacity (mAh) ÷ Power Consumption (mW)
