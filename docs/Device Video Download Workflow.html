Device Video Download Workflow

Download Methods

  1. Automatic Download of Alarm Evidence
  2. Manual Download of Alarm Evidence
  3. Manual Video Clipping Download
  4. Scheduled Task Clipping Download
  5. Download While Viewing and Recording

Instructions for Download Methods

1. Automatic Download of Alarm Evidence

  1. In FTManager ￫ Alarm Management ￫ Alarm Upload, configure the evidence return settings:
    1. set the alarm type return channels for video, images, and black-box data;
    2. select the applicable fleet or vehicle.
  2. When the device triggers the corresponding alarm, a related evidence download task is created, and the evidence video can be viewed in FTVision ￫ Evidence-Alarm.

2. Manual Download of Alarm Evidence

  1. In FTVision ￫ Evidence-Alarm, select an alarm without evidence video and open its details.
  2. In the video section, click Download Video, set the return channel, video duration, and other parameters to create a manual evidence download task.

3. Manual Video Clipping Download

  1. In FTVision ￫ Evidence ￫ Video Playback, select the vehicle device, wait for video loading, click the Clip button in the player controls, fill in the clip name, duration, storage, stream type, etc., to create a clipping task, which is viewable in FTVision ￫ Evidence ￫ Video Library.
  2. In FTVision ￫ Evidence ￫ Video Library, click Create to open the clipping drawer, choose the vehicle device, set the video name, duration, stream type, etc., and create a clipping task that appears in the same Video Library.
  3. In FTVision ￫ Live View ￫ Vehicle Monitoring, use Single Vehicle Escort from the extra actions, wait for the video to load, click the Clip button, configure the video name, duration, stream type, etc., and the task will be visible in the Video Library.

4. Scheduled Task Clipping Download

  1. In FTManager ￫ General Settings ￫ Scheduled Video Download, click Add:
    1. set task name, execution cycle, download time, stream type, upload channel, etc.;
    2. choose the applicable fleets or vehicles.
  2. During the configured execution cycle, video clipping tasks are generated; scheduled downloads can be viewed under Task Details ￫ Task List, and the resulting videos appear in FTVision ￫ Evidence ￫ Video Library.

5. Download While Viewing and Recording

  1. In FTVision ￫ Live View ￫ Vehicle Monitoring, open Live Video for direct streaming; once loaded, click Record while Viewing to start recording, click again to stop, enter a video name in the confirmation popup, and the created clipping task is visible in the Video Library.
